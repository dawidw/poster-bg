Color as emotion

Saturated pairs and one hot accent, not realistic color. A restrained black and white page with a single blue or red does more than a rainbow.

Color in these posters was used emotionally rather than descriptively, from muted tones to stark contrasts. Zamecznik sometimes reduced a poster to lettering on black, the jazz posters set silhouettes against yellow, blue, red and white, and Zofia Stryjeńska had earlier turned folk color into a flat, saturated Slavic art deco. Every palette here has a ground, a dark ink, a light ink and one accent, so a picture can stay quiet until the accent speaks.
