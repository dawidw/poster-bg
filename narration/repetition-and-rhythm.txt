Repetition and rhythm

Multiply, rotate, double: simple forms repeated until they read as music or machinery. Posters for the Warsaw Autumn festival drew sound itself as zigzags and spreading waves.

Zamecznik multiplied, rotated and doubled a form, Świerzy drew rhythmic waves of color for the same festival, Maria Jarema took her rhythm from music and dance, and Wanda Gołkowska built wooden reliefs on rhythm and texture. Here repetition is a parameter: how many copies, what tilt, how tight the spacing, so one form can read as a flower, a fan or an engine.
