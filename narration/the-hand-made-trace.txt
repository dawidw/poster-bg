The hand-made trace

Airbrush fades, slightly off-register inks, torn or cut paper. The small imperfection keeps flat geometry warm.

Hand-printed posters were rarely perfectly aligned, Lenica built his from cut paper, Młodożeniec held bright stains in thick black lines that sat a little off their color, and Magdalena Abakanowicz wove her Abakans into soft forms that hang like fabric. The generator fakes none of it with filters: off-register print, paper grain and ragged cut-outs are all seeded geometry, so a seed still reproduces the exact picture.
