Wojciech Fangor

1922 to 2015, painter, graphic artist, sculptor

Associated with op art and color field painting, and a key figure of Polish postwar abstract art. Between 1953 and 1961 he designed over a hundred posters and co-created the Polish School of Posters.

His mature paintings put pulsating squares and discs of luminous color inside progressively darker rings that softly diffuse into the surrounding field, so the edge seems to breathe. With Stanisław Zamecznik he built Study of Space in 1958, the first spatial installation in Poland.
