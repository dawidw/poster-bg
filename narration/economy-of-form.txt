Economy of form

One theme, two or three elements, a single metaphor. Jan Lenica wrote that for Zamecznik a poster was a mathematical operation: reduce complex formulas to the simplest equation, the sum of two or three elements.

In practice that meant no decoration that did not carry the idea. Józef Mroszczak is remembered for conveying maximum information with minimum means, Katarzyna Kobro built spatial sculptures from a few flat planes in strict architectural order, and the Cyrk circus posters kept to one theme and a metaphor the viewer had to read for themselves.
